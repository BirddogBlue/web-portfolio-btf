White Snow Goose (Domestic Breed)

Overview

The Snow Goose is native to Pennsylvania, but does not stay here long! They nest in sub-arctic climates such as northern Canada, and over-winter in milder regions such as the southern Mid-Atlantic region of the United States.

Domestic White Snow Goose by Branson T Fausey

Originally bred for their meat and egg-laying capabilities, these tall domestic white geese are now seen throughout the United States as well as the UK and Mainland Europe. Primarily, they can be spotted in city parks or even residential backyards, though ducks (both wild and domestic) tend to be more common in both cases. Depending on their environment, these geese may not even mate or lay eggs. If you've seen them before, you've probably seen them in a group, as they tend to hang out with each other rather than with the ducks. However, they don't usually mind sharing space with ducks, and they even often group together with breeds of wild geese, such as the Canada Goose. Be careful when approaching these domestic breeds, as they often associate people with food, and may get aggressive if left unfed! Wild Snow Geese migrate through Pennsylvania each year in the thousands, but these domestic breeds can be seen year round in some locations. Check your local city park. Maybe even a neighboring farm will have some!
